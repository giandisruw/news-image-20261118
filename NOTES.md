## Stories used in the image

### 1. Crimea bridge arch collapses (LEAD)
- Overnight 16–17 Nov: Ukraine struck the Kerch/Crimean bridge for a
  second straight day; video/photos appear to show the road arch span
  collapsed into the Kerch Strait; traffic suspended (road and rail).
- Kyiv: seven-stage operation, "exclusively with Ukrainian weapons" —
  Neptune missiles hit the railway section, Palianytsia drones the road
  section; SBU: one metal arch and the road span "completely destroyed".
- Russia's Defence Ministry: 1,441 drones shot down over 22 regions,
  beating Monday's record 1,336 — the largest Ukrainian drone attack of
  the war. Crimea's Russian-installed governor: 2 killed, traffic halted.
- In reply Russia struck Kyiv's North and South bridges overnight and
  partly damaged the metro; Kyiv mayor confirmed both crossings closed.
- Sources: Reuters (17 Nov), CNN (17 Nov), NY Post (17 Nov),
  Helsinki Times (17 Nov).

### 2. Iran drones target US radar in Bahrain
- Tue 17 Nov: Iran fired drones at a US military radar system in
  Bahrain (IRGC: Sheikh Isa base, home of the US Fifth Fleet) — its
  third attack in recent days on US targets there after a ~two-month
  lull (Fri 13 Nov early-warning radar, Sun 15 Nov al-Rifaa helicopter
  base).
- Bahrain: air defences intercepted the attack after an air-raid alert;
  US military: no damage to US equipment, no injuries to US personnel.
- Context: USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier strike group has arrived in
  the region (US Central Command).
- Sources: Reuters (17 Nov, Nayera Abdallah & Jana Choukeir), AP via
  Military.com (17 Nov), Washington Sun (17 Nov).

### 3. Trump lands in Manila for Quad talks
- Trump arrived late Tue 17 Nov afternoon in Manila at the tail end of
  the 49th ASEAN Summit; first Quad leaders' meeting (US, India, Japan,
  Australia) in two years, and his first as president this term.
- Wed 18 Nov: bilateral talks with President Ferdinand Marcos Jr at
  Malacanang, then on to Shenzhen for the APEC leaders' meeting
  (18–19 Nov) and a planned trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and
  Vladimir Putin on Thu 19 Nov.
- Context in reports: follows the Republicans' midterm election defeat,
  spy allegations, and new US curbs on China's access to AI chips.
- Sources: Reuters (17 Nov, Karen Lema & Mikhail Flores), BBC via
  Rappler, BusinessWorld (17 Nov).

### 4. Hormuz tanker MT Tara Gas hit twice; one sailor missing
- Mon 16 Nov: Panama-flagged LPG tanker MT Tara Gas (24 Indian
  nationals among 25 crew) hit by a missile ~7 nm north of Khasab,
  Oman, near the Strait of Hormuz; engine-room fire.
- Tue 17 Nov: second strike while disabled; crew abandons ship.
  India's MEA condemns the attack: 21 Indians evacuated ashore at
  Khasab, 2 injured in hospital, 1 Indian seafarer missing; search and
  rescue ongoing with Omani authorities. No group has claimed it; no
  government has publicly attributed it.
- Sources: India's Ministry of External Affairs statement (17 Nov),
  IANS (17 Nov), The Business Standard / TBS (17 Nov), UKMTO via
  US News Pulse (17 Nov).

### 5. Knesset picks Eisenkot ally as speaker (+ UK sanctions)
- Tue 17 Nov: Knesset elects Matan Kahana (Yashar, Gadi Eisenkot's
  party) as speaker, 66 votes to Likud's Ofir Katz with 52, replacing
  Amir Ohana. First big move by the Eisenkot-led bloc since Netanyahu
  lost his majority in the 27 Oct election; Eisenkot is trying to form
  a government. Netanyahu remains caretaker PM.
- Tue 17 Nov: Britain sanctions eight Israeli construction/property
  firms building West Bank settlements (asset freezes, director
  disqualifications) plus activist group Tzav 9, shortly before E1
  tender deadlines; Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband: protecting the
  two-state solution is the government's "North Star".
- Sources: Reuters (17 Nov, both stories), CNN (17 Nov), Times of
  Israel via Reuters.

### 6. Cyclone Akvan heads for Andhra coast
- Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal, ~610 km east of Chennai
  on 17 Nov; IMD: intensifies to a Severe Cyclonic Storm on 18 Nov and
  a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm by the morning of 19 Nov.
- Forecast landfall: Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts (Nellore–Tuni /
  Visakhapatnam) around noon Fri 20 Nov, winds 120–130 km/h gusting
  to 145 km/h; heavy rain on the Andhra coast from 18 Nov; fishermen
  told not to venture out 18–20 Nov.
- Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletins (17 Nov),
  Dainik News Live / Jagran Josh (17 Nov).

### 7. Dhoni retires from the IPL
